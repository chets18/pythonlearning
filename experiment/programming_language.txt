previously, i thought vacuum tubes are just like a light bulb using which we can keep one end on while another end off just like a charged head tooth. but i was not completely right. there are  5+ types of vacuum they are, 
• Diode: allow current to flow in only one direction, primarily used for AC-to-DC rectification.
• Triode: Adds a control grid between the cathode and anode to regulate electron flow, enabling signal amplification and switching. (this one is what i talked previously)
• Tetrode: Incorporates a fourth electrode called a screen grid to reduce internal capacitance between the grid and anode, improving high-frequency performance.
• Pentode: Introduces a fifth electrode (the suppressor grid) to prevent secondary electron emission from reaching the screen grid, delivering cleaner and more efficient power amplification.
• Multi-electrode Tubes: Includes hexodes (6 electrodes), heptodes (7 electrodes), and octodes (8 electrodes) designed for specialized high-frequency, mixing, and tuning applications.

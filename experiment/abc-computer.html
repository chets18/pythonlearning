Atanasoff–Berry computer, 1942

Iowa State College · ~300 vacuum tubes · 2 drums × 1,600 capacitors · 30 add-subtract units

The first electronic digital computer. It solved up to 29 simultaneous linear equations by Gaussian elimination, working in pure binary.
